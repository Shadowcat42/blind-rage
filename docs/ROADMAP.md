# Blind Rage roadmap

## In place

- Injected x64 mod with Prism speech (NVDA preferred, other engines as fallback) and one narration pipeline.
- Verified Mark2 disc and title-update profiles sharing one reader.
- Event-driven menu reader: settings, collections, dialogs, records, results, Control Settings and the Custom key editor.
- Subtitles, tutorials, quick-time events, Synchronic count-in, finisher and counter prompts.
- Health, Burst and Unlimited status with warnings.
- Rail-shooting and melee rapid-fire beacons, L1 tracking, boosted lock-on cone and homing.
- Walking-section beacon.
- Controller play (primary) and keyboard play (in progress), following the recomp's own button display and bindings.
- Accessible first-time setup through the PC version's own installer, with spoken progress, and adding the title update or DLC later.
- Mod tutorial boxes using the game's pausing tutorial box: Episode 1's rail-shooting introduction and QTE note.

## Next

- Test Episodes 8 onward and fix what they need.
- Tutorials for the other modes (melee first) and descriptions for fights whose structure isn't obvious, starting with Episode 7's giant Gohma.
- A clearer cue for Episode 5's missile counters, which can come too late to react to.
- Finish keyboard support: lock-on target switching, gesture wording and the right-stick replacement.
- Lighter right-stick browsing in Control Settings.
- Find out why L1 tracking once stayed stuck on an unknown target.
- Versioned adapters for future recomp releases.

Keep new support in the shared readers rather than screen- or boss-specific scripts, verify game behaviour in its scripts and data before relying on it, and test each capability with blind players before claiming it.
